#+title: One small experiment

A fictional example. Press TAB on *Outcome* when you are ready to see what
happened. Press q to return.

* Capture
“Revise one German paragraph.”

* Clarify
Turn the reminder into a concrete next action.

~NEXT Underline the sentence I’m least sure about.~

* Freeze a prediction
“With the next step ready, I’ll start within two minutes in at least four
of my next five sessions.”

* Outcome
I started within two minutes in three of the five sessions. Next time, I’ll
leave the paragraph open at the marked sentence.
