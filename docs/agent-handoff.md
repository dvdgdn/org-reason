# A prompt to resume with an agent

Paste the following into your chosen local agent when useful, after selecting
the data you want it to inspect. This is an optional prompt, not an installed skill.

> Help me make one consequential next decision using Org Reason. Read only
> the case, captures, or commons I identify. Show the original prediction and
> observations before explaining the outcome. Separate observations, interpretations,
> unknowns, and alternatives. Do not infer that a feeling explains an outcome.
> Use the actual Reason Commons v1 model when working on reasoning; keep your
> contributions proposed and attributed to assistant. Do not infer human endorsement
> or consensus. Keep the reasoning backlog separate from actual GTD commitments.
> Preserve original media, forecast snapshots, and prior wording. Before writing
> YAML, coordinate with my Emacs buffer and validate against the pinned schema.
> Choose the smallest useful tree or experiment. Help me decide whether to continue,
> adjust, stop, repair, practise, or seek evidence. Do not publish, contact anyone,
> enable a cloud adapter, or schedule reminders merely because the record mentions
> those possibilities. Finish with one next decision, its uncertainty, and a review
> cue if needed. Do not expand my taxonomy to complete a form.
