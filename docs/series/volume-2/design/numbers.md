# Numbers, dates, names and places: *The Fourth Visit*

Every figure in the story is looked up here. Dates have weekdays and no year.
3 November is a Monday.

## Places

- **Harbor Row Housing**, a nonprofit landlord: 2,140 apartments in 31
  buildings. Office on Tolland Avenue. Depot and stockroom, "the yard", on
  Quarry Road.
- **118 Garnet Street**: 48 apartments. Aldo in 4C, Samira in 2B. Community
  room on the ground floor.
- **Trial buildings** (six): 116, 118 and 120 Garnet Street; 9 and 11 Pell
  Court; 40 Ostrander Avenue. Together 412 apartments, 388 open work orders on
  15 December.
- **St. Brendan's Hospital**, with a rehabilitation wing.
- The port: Odile's control room overlooks the container berths.
- The city's Office of Housing Standards; hearing officer Ms. Petrakis.

- **2019:** the tenant handbook, written by Corinne, promises "someone at your
  door within five working days". The call centre books the next free slot.

  standard van list cut from 140 lines to 85.

## Figures that arrive during the story

- **Notices:** 138 households. By 1 December, 61 written disputes. Complaint
  to the city signed by 212 households.
- **Samira's notebook** (118 Garnet, kept since 1 October): 41 visits coded
  "no access"; someone was home for 29; in 22 of those the van stayed under
  five minutes.
- **Corinne and Mags's sample:** of 60 "no access" visits, 38 had a part on
  order or the wrong trade attached.
- **Wendell's objection:** the backlog began to rise in March, three months
  before the stock cut. Of 150 repeat visits he pulled, 33 (22%) waited on a
  part that used to be on the van.
- **Contractor, two weeks to 1 December:** 310 visits, 107 repairs, 2.9 visits
  per repair, $24,000. They drew parts from the same shelves; in-house
  completions fell from 260 to 215 a week. Backlog on 1 December: 2,015.
  Cancelled on notice: $48,000 spent in all, $48,000 unspent.
- **Compromise week** (seven days, five-minute calls): attended within the
  promise 78%; visits per repair 2.3.
- **Trial, three days at Garnet Street:** attended within five days 58%,
  because 26 jobs were being held for their parts.
- **Bram's arithmetic:** 21 technicians × 4.0 visits × 5 days = 420 visits a
  week; at 1.4 visits per repair, 300 repairs; at 1.2, 350. Against 290 in.
- **Stock for six buildings:** $38,000.
- **Twelve common faults** make up about 60% of reports.
- **Repeat calls** are 35% of all calls.
