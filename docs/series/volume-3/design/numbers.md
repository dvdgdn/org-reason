# Numbers, dates, names, and the fictional records: *Two Minutes at Full Speed*

Dates have weekdays and no year. 5 January is a Monday; 12 March a Thursday.

## Places and people

- **Solberg & Nair**, a two-person translation agency. Client: **Dunmore
  Tool**, worth about $60,000 a year. Visiting: engineers from the machine
  builder **Kessler**, among them **Herr Brandl**.
- The audit: Tuesday 10 to Thursday 12 March, at Dunmore's plant.
- **The Performance Laboratory**, Professor Ambrose Keane; Dr. Ines Albrecht.
- **The German Society**, Aldine Street. Conversation table Thursdays at six,
  run by Jutta Reinholt.
- **Katrin Lohse**, conference interpreter: $4,200 for three days, to be
  booked by Friday 13 February. After that, a colleague at a rush rate of
  $6,800, or half a day for $1,400.
- **The Plasticity Club**, an online group. Wim de Groot.

## The box (chapter 1)

214 saved articles; 31 books; 9 paid courses costing $1,340 in all; 6 apps;
juggling balls; a balance board. Longest run of actual practice: 4 days.

## Her hour

One hour a day, seven hours a week.

Week of 2–8 February, from her log: memory game 2 h 20; flashcards 2 h 30;
ten-second dictation 1 h 10; reading about methods 1 h.

## The check

Four passages of about two minutes on machine tolerances, spoken by Jutta at
full speed as Herr Brandl. Maren gives each in English. Matthias scores ten
points of meaning in each: 40 in all.

- Baseline, Thursday 19 February: 8 of 40.
- Sealed guess, Wednesday 18 February: "On Thursday 5 March I will get at
  least 24 of 40. I am 30% sure." What would count against the practice: 12
  or fewer.
- Result, Thursday 5 March: 23 of 40.

## Image cycling record

Keane: twenty-three cards, nineteen years, every day.

Maren: twenty scenes, written 16–18 January for another purpose. Routine from
Saturday 28 February: ten minutes each morning before the hour. Reported each
day: did it; could she run all twenty without the list; how fast, in her own
words; did she then do the hard exercise.

Sealed with it: "By 12 March the days a week on which I do the hard exercise
will rise from 4 to 6. I am 50% sure. If nothing in my log differs from the
three weeks before, I will say it did nothing I can see."

Result: 6.5 days a week. She cannot separate it from the nearness of the
date. Recorded as untested.
