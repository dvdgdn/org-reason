* Your trusted system — Step 3: Clarify

/Ten minutes./

Take the first ten lines of your captured list, in order. Don't skip the ugly
ones. For each, ask two questions.

1. *What would be true when this is finished?* Write a result someone else
   could check.
2. *What is the very next thing I would physically do?* Start with a verb:
   call, send, look up, draft. If you can't picture your hands doing it, it is
   still too big.

Then sort each line one of four ways:

- It takes under two minutes: do it now.
- It is one step: write the step on a new list titled *Next Actions*.
- It needs nothing done: keep it as reference, or cross it out.
- It needs several steps, someone else, or a particular day: put a mark beside
  it and leave it. Steps 4, 5 and 7 deal with those.

* The same step in Org Reason

Press ~C-c r i~ to open the inbox, put point on the first heading and press
~C-c r x~. The first prompt is the sort above. ~M-?~ at any prompt explains it.

| Your line is                    | Choose        | Then                                                     |
|---------------------------------+---------------+----------------------------------------------------------|
| One step you can take           | =next-action= | Rewrite it as the physical action; pick a context        |
| Done in two minutes, just now   | =done-now=    | Do it first. The command asks if you really did          |
| Worth keeping, nothing to do    | =reference=   | Your list of entrances from step 1 belongs here          |
| A worry or a question           | =feeling=, =question= | Kept as a note, never shown as a task            |
| Nothing                         | =discard=     | Moved aside to =archive/discarded.org=, not deleted      |

The context (=@calls=, =@computer=, =@errands= …) says where the action is
possible. Effort is optional; leave it blank if you don't know.

Leave the several-step, waiting and dated items in the inbox for now.

- [ ] ~C-c r a~ shows your new actions under NEXT, each starting with a verb.
- [ ] The inbox count on ~C-c r d~ has gone down by about ten.

* Carry forward

A Next Actions list, and an inbox that still holds the bigger items. Clarify a
few more lines whenever you have five minutes. The next four steps empty the
rest.
