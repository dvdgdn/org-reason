* In the story

A sponsor's photograph is blocking the cart, and guests for the second
reception are lining up outside in the wind. Maya knows exactly what to say to
move the photograph. Eli calls across the room to ask whether she can open the
second list. Moving the photograph is his job, and he can do it. Only she is
free to work the door. She goes to the door. Earlier she had tried to hand a
driver an address that Eli had already given him; she folded her copy and put
it away.

* Your trusted system — Step 16: Engage

/Five minutes now, then all day./

The lists show you your options. You still have to choose among them, and
choosing well is a skill of its own.

When you have a gap, read your Next Actions and narrow them with four
questions, in this order:

1. *Where am I, and what do I have with me?* Drop what is impossible here.
2. *How long do I have?* Drop what won't fit.
3. *How much energy do I have?* Drop what you would do badly.
4. *Of what is left, which matters most, and which can only I do?*

Then do that one thing and no other.

Two checks from the story:

- When something goes wrong in front of you, ask whose it is before you step
  in. If you handed it over in step 14, leave it with them.
- When you catch yourself repeating an instruction someone already has, stop.
  Fold the paper and put it away.

Try it once today. Write down what you chose and what you left alone.

* The same step in Org Reason

Press ~C-c r e~ and answer three prompts.

| Prompt            | Answer                                                    |
|-------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------|
| Context           | Where you are: =@computer=, =@calls=, =@errands=. Blank for any |
| Minutes available | A whole number. Blank for any                             |
| Energy            | =low=, =medium= or =high=. Blank for any                  |

The buffer lists the NEXT actions that fit. Actions tagged =@anywhere= always
appear. Actions with no estimate stay visible and are never assumed to be
quick. The fourth question is not asked, because the priority is yours to
decide. Follow a link with ~C-c C-o~, do the action, then ~C-c C-t~ and DONE.

Two details make the filter worth trusting:

- Each action needs a context that is really a constraint. ~C-c C-q~ on an
  action changes its tags.
- To filter by energy, give demanding actions the property =OR_ENERGY= with
  the value =high=: ~C-c r M~ on the action's heading sets it.

What you handed over in step 14 is not in this list. It is in the WAIT block,
under the other person's name.

- [ ] You can name one thing you saw and deliberately left to its owner.
