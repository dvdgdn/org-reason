* In the story

Before noon Maya can buy one thing, the room, so she signs for it and leaves
the price of a second service open until Monday. She draws a line through
=Find a room= and writes the work that is left: =Serve both groups dinner=.
She blocks half an hour to look through her week's pages. A phone call takes
it.

* Your trusted system — Step 7: Calendar

/Ten minutes./

The calendar holds only what has to happen on a certain day or at a certain
time. If you also fill it with things you merely hope to do, you stop
believing it.

1. Go through your captured list, Projects and Waiting For. Move onto the
   calendar every appointment, every deadline someone else has set, and every
   time you have promised an answer ("I'll call you by lunchtime").
2. Take off the calendar anything that isn't tied to its date. Those are next
   actions. Maya's coffee order sat at six o'clock for no reason she could
   remember.
3. When a project's outcome is reached, cross it off the Projects list. If
   work remains, give that work its own name as a new project.
4. Block thirty minutes this week to look over all your lists. Treat it as an
   appointment with someone you would not cancel on.

* The same step in Org Reason

Your appointments stay in whatever calendar you already use. Org Reason adds
dated commitments to its own agenda.

- A commitment for a day or time :: ~C-c r x~ on the inbox item, choose
  =calendar=, then give the date, with a time if there is one
  (=2026-11-14 11:30=). It moves to =gtd/calendar.org=.
- Something to bring back later :: choose =tickler= and a date. On that day
  the agenda shows it again. A tickler is a reminder to reconsider, with no
  commitment yet.
- A real deadline on a project or action :: ~C-c C-d~ on its heading.
- A finished project :: ~C-c C-t~ on the project heading, choose DONE. Capture
  the remaining work with ~C-c r C~ and clarify it as a new =project=.
- The review block :: ~C-c r C~, type =Look over all my lists=, choose
  =calendar=, give the day and time.

Ordinary next actions get no date. The agenda's first block then shows only
what truly belongs to each day.

- [ ] ~C-c r a~ shows this week with only real commitments on it.
- [ ] The inbox (~C-c r i~) is empty, or close to it, for the first time.

* Carry forward

A calendar you can believe, an empty inbox, and a half-hour appointment with
your lists. If the appointment gets eaten, as Maya's did, move it to a new
time straight away so it doesn't just disappear.
