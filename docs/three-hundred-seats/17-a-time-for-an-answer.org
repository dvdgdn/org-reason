* In the story

The invoices go into a labelled envelope. Maya crosses off =call Maren=: what
she wanted from the call was reassurance, and that was never an action. Then a
stranger phones to ask whether she can confirm the same hall for the spring.
Maya can see the empty Saturday in her memory, and she doesn't answer from it.
She asks what the room has to do, says "I can give you a status by two
tomorrow", and stays in the car until the call is in her notebook and two
checks are on the calendar.

* Your trusted system — Step 17: Maintain

/Ten minutes, then a little every week./

A system built in a crisis usually stops being used when the crisis ends. Four
habits keep it going.

1. *Capture at the moment of the promise.* Before you leave the car, the room
   or the call, write it down. This is step 1 again, as a reflex.
2. *Answer with a time when you don't have the fact.* Say "I'll tell you by
   two tomorrow", then put both the check and the reply on your calendar. Don't
   confirm anything from memory.
3. *File what is finished.* Label it so you can find it next year, and take it
   off your lists.
4. *Review every week.* Take the time you measured in step 11, pick a day and
   an hour, and put it on the calendar as a repeating appointment.

Last, read your lists once more and cross off anything that is a wish. You
can only do actions.

You now have one inbox, six lists, a calendar and a review. Read the story's
first chapter again and count how many of them would have caught Tuesday.

* The same step in Org Reason

- The reflex :: ~C-c r C~ captures and clarifies in one motion. For the call
  in the story it is used three times: =Anika — date and room requirements=
  as =delegate=; =Ask Jo what Weller has in March= as =next-action= with
  =@calls=; and =Give Anika a status= as =calendar= at =tomorrow 14:00=. If
  there is no time even for that, ~C-c r c~ takes one line and asks nothing.
- The weekly review :: open =gtd/calendar.org= and add a heading =Weekly
  review= (~M-RET~). Press ~C-c C-s~ and give the day and time. Then edit the
  timestamp it inserts so that it ends in =+1w=, for example
  =<2026-11-13 Fri 16:00 +1w>=. It now appears in the agenda every week. When
  it comes up, ~C-c r w~ opens that week's checklist, and marking the entry
  DONE moves it to the following week.
- Filing :: clarify finished paperwork as =reference= (~C-c r x~), or import
  the file itself with ~C-c r m~. ~C-c r /~ with =OR_TYPE= and =reference=
  finds it later.
- Wishes :: for an entry that was never an action, ~C-c C-t~ and CANCELLED,
  with a word about what you really wanted.

Then make the dashboard (~C-c r d~) the first thing you open each morning.
When it shows no unclarified captures, no stalled projects and no nameless
waits, the system is working.

- [ ] A repeating =Weekly review= entry is in the agenda.
- [ ] The next promise you make aloud is in the inbox before you stand up.
