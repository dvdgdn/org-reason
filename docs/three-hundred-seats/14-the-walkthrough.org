* In the story

The walkthrough and the recital are at the same hour. Maya first offers to
join by video and sign afterwards. Eli asks, "Who says it's fine?" So she
gives him the decision: a hundred and sixty or fewer, the lane from the ramp
and the kitchen kept open, two hundred dollars he can spend without calling,
and don't sign if it can't be made to work. She tells the others before they
can ask her. Then she sits in the chair with her name on it and puts her phone
away.

* Your trusted system — Step 14: Delegate the decision as well

/Ten minutes./

If you hand over a task but keep the decision, you still have to be there.
Hand over both, and say where the edges are.

Choose one thing this week that someone else could decide. Write five lines.

1. *Outcome:* what has to be true at the end.
2. *Limits:* what must not change, in numbers where you can.
3. *Means:* the money, time or tools they may use without asking.
4. *Stop:* the point at which they do not go ahead, and what they bring back to
   you.
5. *Report:* what you want to hear afterwards, and when.

Give the five lines to the person. Then tell everyone else involved who decides
now. Do it before the moment arrives, so nobody phones you in the middle.

Put the report on your Waiting For list. Then be unavailable. If someone asks
you anyway, the answer is the person's name.

* The same step in Org Reason

1. Press ~C-c r C~ and type the outcome as one line, for example
   =Eli — signed final floor plan for Weller=. Choose =delegate= and give the
   name.
2. Open the entry from the agenda (~C-c r a~, ~RET~). Under the heading, write
   the other four lines:
   #+begin_example
   Limits: 160 or fewer per sitting; lane from ramp and kitchen stays open.
   Means: $200 setup allowance, no need to call.
   Stop: if it can't work inside that, don't sign; tell me what would change.
   Report: photo of the signed plan, Thursday evening.
   Told: Jo, Denise, Ruth — Thursday 4 pm.
   #+end_example
3. ~C-c C-s~ on the heading sets when you expect the report.
4. Send the brief yourself. The entry is your record of what you said.

When the report comes, read the =Limits= line against it before you mark the
entry DONE.

- [ ] One WAIT entry carries an outcome, limits, means, a stop rule and a
  report time.
- [ ] The =Told:= line names everyone who might otherwise have asked you.

* Carry forward

One decision that someone else now owns, and an hour of your own given back.
Notice what you did with the hour.
