dinner as sold would leave the pantry short even if every guest came.
Receptions with refunds on offer might not. Ruth asks what happens at thirty

It lasts about as long as it takes Ruth to fold a schedule.

* Your trusted system — Step 12: Purpose

/Ten minutes./

A project is a way of getting something that matters more than the project
itself. When the two come apart, keep the purpose and change the project.

1. On a new page titled *What this is for*, list the few things your projects
   serve: areas you are responsible for, and goals for this year. Five to ten
   lines.
2. Beside each project on your Projects list, write which line it serves. A
   project that serves none is a candidate for Someday / Maybe, or for
   stopping.
3. Pick the project in most trouble. Write its purpose on a slip of paper and
   put it where you can see it. Ask: is the plan as it stands still the best
   way to reach this? What else would?
4. Write the number at which the answer changes: "This works if no more than
   __." Then write what you will do above that number.

Decide while the people who depend on you can still adjust.

* The same step in Org Reason

1. Open =gtd/horizons.org= in your Org Reason directory (~C-x C-f~). Add a
   heading for each area of responsibility or goal: ~M-RET~, type it, repeat.
2. Open each project from ~C-c r a~ and extend the =For:= line from step 4 so
   it names one of those headings.
3. A project that serves nothing: ~C-c C-t~ on its heading and choose SOMEDAY to
   set it aside, or CANCELLED to stop it. Either one asks nothing more of you
   and keeps the record.
4. For the threshold in item 4, write it under the project heading:
   =Works if refunds ≤ 36. Above that: combine the Friday runs.=

*Optional: keep the forecast honest.* A threshold like that is a prediction, and
Org Reason can hold you to it. ~C-c r p~ drafts an experiment: give it a title,
the prediction ("No more than 36 of 300 will ask for a refund") and a review
date. With point on its top heading, ~C-c r f~ freezes the wording so it cannot
be quietly improved afterwards. Step 15 compares it with what happened. Lesson
7 of the [[file:../user-guide.org][user guide]] covers this in full.

- [ ] =gtd/horizons.org= has five to ten headings.
- [ ] Every active project names what it is for, and one has a threshold.

* Carry forward

A purpose behind every project, and one plan you have decided to change. Other
people were promised the old plan, and they have not been told yet.
