* In the story

Early on Tuesday, before anyone else is in, Maya lays out the notebook, the
laptop, the envelope and a paper bag from her coat pocket. She leaves the
unread mail alone and opens the calendar first. She deletes a reminder about a
hall she no longer has, sends one two-minute email, and parks an idea for next
year on a back page. Then she finds two deadlines eight lines apart, and the
earlier one is tomorrow. Because she has seen it in time, she can rethink what
she is offering.

* Your trusted system — Step 11: The first full review

/Fifteen to thirty minutes. This is the step that makes the rest work./

Do it somewhere quiet, in this order. Don't answer messages while you do it.

1. *Gather.* Empty pockets, bags, loose paper and phone notes into your capture
   place. Clarify each item as in steps 3–7.
2. *Calendar, back two weeks.* Does anything need a follow-up?
3. *Calendar, ahead four weeks.* What needs preparing? Check each deadline
   against its source document. Look for an earlier deadline hiding behind the
   one you wrote down.
4. *Next Actions.* Cross off what is done. Remove what no longer matters.
5. *Waiting For.* Does every line have a name and a check time? Is any overdue?
6. *Projects.* For each one: is the outcome still what I want, and what is its
   live step?
7. *Someday / Maybe.* Promote one item or delete one.
8. *Collisions.* Is there any day on which you have promised to be in two
   places?

Finish by writing one thing you now see that you didn't see before.

* The same step in Org Reason

Press ~C-c r w~. It opens this week's review file, =gtd/reviews/<year>-W<week>.org=,
with a checklist in the same order: Get clear, Get current, Get creative.
~C-c C-c~ on a line ticks its box. The file keeps your answers; reopening it
later in the week continues where you stopped.

Work with two views beside it:

- ~C-c r d~, the dashboard, for the inbox count, projects without a live step,
  and waiting entries without a person. Aim for zero, "None found", "None
  found".
- ~C-c r a~, the agenda, for the calendar, NEXT, WAIT, projects and SOMEDAY, in
  that order. In the agenda, ~b~ and ~f~ move back and forward a week.

Org Reason cannot see your paper, your email or your outside calendar. The
checklist's first lines remind you to look at them yourself.

Under =One decision for next week=, write the thing you now see. Under =What to
defer or stop=, write at least one line. Skip the optional learning section
this time.

- [ ] Inbox at zero, no stalled projects, a name on every WAIT.
- [ ] The review file has one decision and one thing to stop.

* Carry forward

Lists that are true as of today, a decision, and a rough idea of how long a
review takes you. You will need that number in step 17.
