Org Reason bookmarklet

Drag the button onto your bookmarks bar. On any page, select text (or nothing) and click it.

Org Reason

The selection, or just the link when nothing is selected, goes straight into the Org Reason inbox, unclassified, titled with the page title. Clarify it later with C-c r x.

Emacs must be running a server, and your OS must pass org-protocol:// links to emacsclient: see “Browser capture” in docs/install.md. The first time a site sends a capture, the browser asks to open the handler; allow it for that site. Only the page URL, title and selection are sent, only when you click, and only to your own Emacs.
